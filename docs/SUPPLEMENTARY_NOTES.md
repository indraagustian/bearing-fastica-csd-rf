### B. Split-First Per-Fold Preprocessing FastICA Ablation (`results/results_split_first_raw.csv`)

Each 18,000-sample recording is split into five 3,600-sample contiguous blocks *before* fitting `FastICA`, CSD coherence alignment, and `StandardScaler` exclusively on training blocks:

- `Without FastICA` vs `With FastICA`:
  - **Random Forest (Regularized):** `50.23% ± 4.15%` -> **`61.53% ± 3.25%`** (**+11.30 pp**)
  - **Random Forest (Nested-CV):** `55.97% ± 5.30%` -> **`64.61% ± 1.47%`** (**+8.64 pp**)
  - Top split-first classifiers with FastICA: `SVM (RBF)` (`67.16% ± 2.84%`, AUC `0.910`), `MLP (ANN)` (`64.85% ± 1.21%`, AUC `0.901`), `Random Forest (Tuned)` (`64.61% ± 1.47%`, AUC `0.905`).

## 3. Gini Feature Importance & Cross-Version Note

Three raw recordings (`Tanpa_Pelumas_posisi_{1,2,3}.csv`, 18,000 samples each) are included under `data/raw_fieldtest/`. They document the dry-lubrication test condition collected after observing upward severity shifts on unseen 2-ball and 3-ball removal evaluations under grease loss and lubricant drying.
